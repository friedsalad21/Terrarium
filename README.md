# Terrarium

**▶ Play it live: https://friedsalad21.github.io/Terrarium/**

A living planet sealed inside a lab observation hall (or a glass tank or globe on a desk), with a tiny artificial sun. Every world starts as molten rock. It cools, rain fills the oceans, and nothing lives until you drop a payload of microbes into the sea. Blooms spread through the water, plants green the coasts, and animals evolve through genes and natural selection. Smart species light fires, unite their tribes into kingdoms and empires, wage wars, and climb from the Stone Age to the Space Age.

God tools let you send rain, meteors and new species, plant a monolith, or appear as a god to the people below, who build temples and leap ahead. A drone camera dives down to follow a single creature or town up close, and a control-room readout shows how fast time runs inside (1 s outside = N years inside).
