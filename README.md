# Terrarium

**▶ Play it live: https://friedsalad21.github.io/Terrarium/**

A living planet sealed inside a lab observation hall (or a glass tank, a glass globe, or floating free in space), with a tiny artificial sun. Every world starts as molten rock. It cools, rain fills the oceans, and nothing lives until you drop a payload of microbes into the sea. Blooms spread through the water, plants green the coasts, and animals evolve through genes and natural selection. Smart species light fires, unite their tribes into kingdoms and empires, wage wars, and climb from the Stone Age to the Space Age.

Zoom all the way down to the ground. The planet is shaded on the GPU: mountains, beaches, forests, snow, oceans with waves and sun glints, cloud shadows and city lights. A drone camera dives to follow a single animal or town. Up close you see four-legged animals and upright people walking, forests of trees, huts, houses, towers with lit windows, castles and golden temples.

God tools let you send rain, meteors and new species, plant a monolith, or appear as a god to the people below. Slow motion (⅛×, ½×) lets you watch the surface in real time; the control-room readout shows how fast time runs inside (1 s outside = N years inside). Click a Chronicle entry to fly there.

Keys: 1-8 tools, D drone, [ ] slower/faster, Space pause, H hide panel, Esc back.
